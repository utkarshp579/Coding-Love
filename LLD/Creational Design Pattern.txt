They help in managing how bojects are created. 

Instead of creating object all over your code,
Creational patterns provide a smart , controlled way to handle the object creation process.


They help centralize , & stramline object creation. 
    reducing duplication of work by making them abstract.

There are 5 famous creational pattern.

1. Singleton Patterns :- V.V.imp
- Think of it like the factory's manager
- ensure that only one person is incharge of making the most important product.
it ensures only one instance of a class throughout the entire system.
- this prevent resource wastage & ensure consistent behavior
Ex - A single database connection shared across the whole system. 


2. Factory Method pattern :- Imp.
-  think of it as an automatic assembly line
- the factory knows how to produce different types of products
instead of specifying the exact product type, you simply call the factory method to handle it.
Example :- A car factory that creates different car models dynamically

3. Abstract Factory Patterns : Not that important
- Think of it as a Mega Factory
- the factory produces multiple related products
- instead of creating a single product , it creates families of objects ( like an entire furniture set). 
Ex - A furniture factory that creates chairs , tables , and sofas together
    Here table could be a factory creating different kinds of table.

4. Builder Pattern :- V. imp
Think of it as custom order system 
When creating complex products ( like a custom - built car ) you don't want to handle it one go.
the builder pattern lets you break down the creation process into steps.
- Ex : Building a luxury car with step by step feature.


5. Prototype Pattern
think of it as copying a ready-made product
- instead of creating objects from scratch, you clone an existing one.
saves time and resources.
- creating clones of games character in a game development system,j



why to use?
- simplify the object creation
